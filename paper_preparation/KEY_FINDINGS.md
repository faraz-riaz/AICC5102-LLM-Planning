# Ranked findings and interpretation instructions

Use these as writing bullets; do not paste them as a finished paper. All gap percentages use `100*(L-L*)/L*` on valid plans with proven L*>0. Source aliases are defined in EVIDENCE_INVENTORY.md.

1. **High coverage does not imply optimal plans.** Archived R1 A* solves **65/90**, compared with hmax **14/90**, but is suboptimal on **5/17** exact-reference solved tasks (mean gap **2.7895%**, maximum **16.6667%**). Initial R1 estimates exceed L* on **15/17** easy tasks, median ratio **1.1765**. Sources: RUNS + REFS, ALL90 `quality.csv`, generated A11. Main §5.2–5.3, F2. Interpretation: useful budgeted guidance without admissibility. Limit: exact quality and accuracy evidence is easy-only; coexistence is not a causal analysis.

2. **WA* improves coverage, with little median paired effort change.** R1 solves **70/90** at w=1.5, **69/90** at w=2, versus A* **65/90**. Medium counts are **30/30**, **29/30**, **25/30** respectively. WA*/A* median expansion ratios are **1.00** on **65** common solutions for either weight. Sources: RUNS; PAIRS `paired_vs_astar.csv`; A1. Main §5.2, F1. Interpretation: observed coverage benefit under the fixed contract, not a general speedup. No repeats or inference that 1.5 is universally best; weighting was a fixed two-point comparison.

3. **IDA* has a pronounced quality cost with this LLM heuristic.** Easy coverage **24/30** versus A* **30/30**; on the same **17** exact-reference tasks, mean gap **21.8278%**, median **17.6471%**, maximum **100%**, optimal **2/17**. IDA* costs more than A* on **21/24** commonly solved easy tasks. Sources: EASY30 `quality.csv`, PAIRS `paired_vs_astar.csv`. Main §5.2, F2. IDA* starts its bound at h(s0), making a high initial bound a plausible explanation; successor ordering, path-only duplicate checking and heuristic values also matter. No component experiment isolates a cause.

4. **The original coverage ranking is reproduced under a reduced budget.** Same-machine GBFS R1/hFF/hmax solve **64/38/19** of 90. R1/hFF domain ratios **2.50/1.45/1.50**, supporting the published direction in all three domains. Sources: BASE, current `reproduction.csv`, published Table 2 cells verified in T1. Main §5.1, T1. Amina's historical **53/25/14** is reported separately and is not independently raw-verified here. The interpreter, resource budget and test subset differ from the original study; do not explain discrepancies by one factor alone.

5. **The benefit degrades sharply at higher difficulty.** R1 A* solves **30/30 easy, 25/30 medium, 10/30 hard**; hmax solves **14/30, 0/30, 0/30**. WA1.5 recovers all medium tasks but only **10/30 hard**. Source: ALL90 `coverage_by_difficulty.csv`, A1/A9. Main F1 and §5.2; this supplies the assignment's harder-condition experiment now. Timeouts describe the budget, not unsolvability. New-trial scaling is still pending R7.

6. **A* only modestly improves archived-LLM quality over GBFS.** Mean gap moves from **3.3098% to 2.7895%** on the same **17** easy tasks: **0.5204 percentage points**. Across **63** common solutions of any reference status, A* is cheaper on **19**, equal on **36**, costlier on **8**; GBFS minus A* mean difference **0.3333 actions**, median expansion ratio **1.00**. Sources: REFS+RUNS; PAIRS `paired_vs_astar.csv`. Main §5.2; exact-gap and raw-cost comparisons have different denominators and must be identified.

7. **Weight-insensitive exact gaps do not imply identical plans or costs everywhere.** Both WA* weights match A*'s gaps on **17** proven tasks. On **65** common solved tasks they have equal costs on **59**, costlier plans on **6**, and identical plan hashes on **57**. Mean extra cost is **0.1692 actions** at 1.5 and **0.2000** at 2.0. Sources: PAIRS `paired_vs_astar.csv`, A4. Main one qualification adjacent to finding 2; detailed pairs appendix. The extra-cost tasks have no proven optimum, so avoid exact-gap claims there.

8. **IDA* trades recorded memory for repeated work, particularly with hmax.** On **7** matched hmax easy tasks, median IDA*/A* expansion ratio **45.0804**, wall-time ratio **63.8568**, median iterations **8**, same optimal cost on all seven. R1 median expansion ratio **1.1926** on **24** tasks, but maximum **2203.98**; median iterations **1**. Recorded IDA* peak-memory median **14.15 MiB** over its easy attempts (maximum R1 **22.6 MiB**). Sources: PAIRS `ida_vs_astar.csv`, RUNS. Main short statement, F3/A7/A10 appendix. Do not compare easy-only IDA* maxima with all90 A* maxima as a matched memory saving; process memory includes grounding.

9. **Recorded failures and measurement limits matter to interpretation.** Of **780** runs: **363 success, 404 timeout, 13 memory limit**; zero invalid plans, errors or exhaustive unsolved outcomes. Zero observed violations among audited proof-eligible guarantees; A*+hmax **14/14** and IDA*+hmax **7/7** reference-known solved tasks are optimal; two WA2+hmax suboptimal results remain within w-bound. Sources: RUNS+REFS, AUDIT and A8/A9. Main setup/limitations, detailed appendix. Report zero violations with eligibility, not as a universal proof. Grounding explanations in integration prose need their dedicated measurement logs; empty worker logs do not prove phase attribution.

## Claims to reserve or reject

- R6 sampled-state frequency, R8 three-trial mean ± SD and R9 task-level error/quality association are unmeasured. The three archived domain files are one selected set, not three independent trials.
- hmax estimates are lower bounds; never compute an exact gap or overestimation frequency using hmax as h*.
- Cross-domain gap averages over these 17 tasks are dominated by Miconic (10/17); report the domain distribution and selection bias.
- Search wall times exclude/includes different portions of execution than GBFS CPU search time; use total wall times for pairs and explain startup/grounding overhead. Median runtime parity is not evidence of equal search cost.
- The observed near-greedy expansion behavior is consistent with heuristic magnitude dominating g; no rescaling/component-removal experiment was done. Label this explanation as a hypothesis.
- Do not claim priority. The verified October 2026 LeanPlan preprint directly addresses LLM heuristics and admissibility; use the related-work verification notes before positioning the extension.
